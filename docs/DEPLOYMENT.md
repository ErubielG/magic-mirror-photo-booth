# Deployment record

The Magic Mirror Photo Booth operated at a California quinceañera on November 14, 2026. Booth19 was initially installed, then **booth20 was installed as a rapid update during setup and the arrival of the first guests**. The updated booth operated autonomously for the remainder of the evening; guests captured images and requested prints without operator intervention. For each request, the Raspberry Pi validated and composed up to four session photos into a 4×6-inch sheet, then sent the job directly over the PhotoBooth Wi-Fi network to the **Canon SELPHY CP1500** using CUPS (`lp -d selphy`). The CP1500 had been manually connected to the PhotoBooth network before the event. Both manual printing and operator approval were disabled. No phone-mediated printing or `/admin` approval was used during the event.

The author recalls bringing approximately 30–40 sheets of photo paper, with none left unused at the end of the event. Paper depletion is evidence of consumption, not an exact, independently verified successful print count. This remains a single field event, not a statistically measured reliability trial; see [event data recovery](EVENT_DATA_RECOVERY.md). No private event photo archive is stored in this repository.
